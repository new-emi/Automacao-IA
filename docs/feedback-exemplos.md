# Exemplos de Feedback dos Destinatários

Modelos de respostas coletáveis via formulário, usados para calibrar o instrumento de coleta e ilustrar o tipo de percepção do público-alvo.

## Feedbacks positivos

1. "These morning emails have become part of my routine. Seeing my name, a short reflection and a beautiful image starts my day with intention. Thank you for the consistency!"
2. "I love the Monday 'A Fresh Start' messages — they set the tone for my whole week. The personal touch of seeing my name makes it feel like it was written just for me."
3. "The Sunday reflections are my favorite. They help me slow down and prepare for the week ahead. Please never stop sending them!"
4. "As a social work intern, the daily messages give me language and ideas I can share with my own clients. It's like a small supervision gift in my inbox."
5. "Short, warm, and always on time. Exactly what I need on a Monday morning. Thank you!"
